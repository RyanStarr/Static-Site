The home lab

An OPNsense router, a Proxmox cluster and a TrueNAS server, with an NGINX reverse proxy in front of the services and Tailscale so I can reach them from anywhere. On top of that runs Nextcloud, which holds the family photos, and a voice assistant built from Home Assistant, Ollama, Whisper and Piper, all on my own hardware rather than someone else's cloud. Uptime Kuma keeps an eye on all of my services and pings me on Discord when something fails.

Most of what I know about infrastructure I learned by running this, breaking it, and having to get it back up.
