I'm a junior system administrator, and no two days look quite the same. On a typical
one I'm building out our OpenStack cluster, patching the services already running on
it, or fitting new hardware into the rack. When something needs deploying, it goes out
through a CI/CD pipeline rather than by hand.

I also look after everything people actually touch day to day: their MacBooks, Wi-Fi,
and the kit on their desks. When someone new starts, I'm the one enrolling them into our
central user database so they can log in on their first morning.

The services I keep running include GitLab, OpenStack and a fair amount of Docker. I set
up our single sign-on service and have been connecting everything else to it, one
service at a time, so people have one login instead of a dozen.

The biggest things I've built so far are a full OpenStack cluster, taken from start to
finish, and a Proxmox cluster backed by Ceph storage.
